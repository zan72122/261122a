## Original Procedural Assets
- **Cordless Cyclonic Stick Vacuum & First-Person Hand** (`js/vacuum.js`, `js/textures.js`) — Built procedurally in Three.js with Parallel-Transport (Bishop-frame) lofted anatomical hand & draped knit sleeve geometry, beveled industrial components, procedural PBR micro-textures, and CC0 `cotton_jersey` knit sleeve textures.
- **Living Room Details & Debris** (`js/room.js`, `js/debris.js`, `js/textures.js`) — Procedural woven flatweave rug albedo & interactive cotton fringe tassels, framed minimalist botanical linen wall art, clearing sandy floor dust smudges, breadcrumbs, sand grit, torn paper scraps, and hidden under-sofa brass coins.
- **Procedural Web Audio** (`js/audio.js`) — Synthesized in real time via Web Audio API (filtered pink noise airflow, brushless motor hum & turbine whine, breadcrumb tube clatter, sand crackle, paper fwoop, coin metallic clink, mechanical latch click, cascading dump patter, lid snap, croissant crunch, plant leaf rustle, hollow rattan basket knock, sofa thump, and clean room chime).
